Help & Support

Find answers to your questions or reach out for assistance

How do I create a new class?
To create a new class, use the "Create Class" button on your main Dashboard. Provide the class details, and a unique 6-character class code will be automatically generated. Share this code with your students so they can join.
How can I mark student attendance?
Inside any classroom view, go to the "Attendance" tab on the left menu. Here you will find a roster of joined students where you can update their present or absent status for specific dates.
How do I upload and manage student marks?
Navigate to the "Marks" tab within a classroom. You can view the list of students and directly input their scores for different assessments like mid-terms, teacher assessments, and end-semester exams.
How do I communicate with my students?
Use the "Class Chat" option located in the classroom sidebar. You can send general announcements, course materials, or reply to student queries. Messages here are broadcasted to all enrolled students.
